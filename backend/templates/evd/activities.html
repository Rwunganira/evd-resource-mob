How to read this table
Govt Cost — planned budget for the activity.
Committed — total partners have pledged (from Partner Contributions).
Disbursed — funds partners have actually transferred to government.
Budget Executed — what government has spent (set via the pencil icon).
Commitment Gap — Govt Cost − Committed (still to be pledged).
Disbursement Gap — Govt Cost − Disbursed (still to be received).
Cov % — Committed ÷ Govt Cost.
Exec % — Budget Executed ÷ Disbursed — spend efficiency (a badge over 100% means more was spent than received).
